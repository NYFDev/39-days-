# NYF Niche Publishing Network

Status: proposed build track

## Objective

Build a small network of tightly focused publishing properties that turn NYF's existing research and writing corpus into searchable, useful public resources. Each property should own a narrow subject territory, publish substantive material, earn organic search visibility, and route interested readers into the larger NYF ecosystem.

This is not a thin-content or mass-site strategy. The operating rule is: retrieval before reconstruction, substance before scale.

## Why this belongs in NYF

NYF already has multiple bodies of work that can support distinct editorial verticals, including:

- East Corner / diaspora and East African intelligence
- Alchemical Protocols / financial structure, tax mechanics, valuation, collateralization and wealth architecture
- Pattern Recognition / Dusty Philosopher synthesis work
- 39 DAYS / execution and documentary chronology
- Ten Cots / long-form documentary and memoir work
- Research Desk / source-verified research themes

The opportunity is to map the existing corpus to searchable subject territories instead of forcing every idea into one general corporate site.

## Publishing model

For each proposed property:

1. Retrieve the existing corpus first.
2. Define one narrow search territory and audience.
3. Build a content inventory from existing work, fragments, research and finished pieces.
4. Identify gaps only after retrieval.
5. Publish a small number of high-substance pages before scaling.
6. Measure search impressions, clicks, engagement and repeat readership.
7. Add monetization only after the property demonstrates genuine audience value.
8. Route readers back to relevant NYF projects, newsletters, tools or research.

## Monetization paths

Potential monetization includes display advertising such as Google AdSense after publisher approval, affiliate relationships where appropriate, sponsorship, subscriptions, data products, research products and NYF-owned applications.

Traffic alone does not guarantee monetization. Each property must satisfy the policies and quality requirements of any advertising or affiliate platform it uses.

## Initial vertical candidates

### 1. Alchemical Protocols

Search territory: financial mechanics that are usually discussed separately but operate as one system.

Core themes:

- tax-return mechanics and line-item interpretation
- corporate structure
- collateralization and cross-collateralization
- distributions, dividends and returns
- enterprise and brand valuation
- equity creation
- financing and investment strategy
- case studies such as the Kylie Jenner effect
- the McKinley General Factor dissertation/research line

This vertical should be built from the recovered six-document/app layer and dissertation corpus rather than from newly invented summaries.

### 2. East Corner

Search territory: East African and Somali-diaspora enterprise, capital, culture, infrastructure and overlooked builders.

Use the historical Eastcorner corpus, Custodian Collective/Claw-bot research architecture, and the current East Corner publication layer as source material.

### 3. Pattern Recognition

Search territory: synthesis essays that connect behavioral, cultural, financial and systems ideas.

Seed material includes the Dusty Philosopher / Raw Story Movement work and the existing Pattern Recognition lines.

## Architecture options

Do not choose WordPress merely because it is familiar. Evaluate the lowest-friction publishing stack for each vertical. WordPress is one candidate because it can support independent domains, editorial workflows, feeds, structured metadata and ad integrations. Static sites or other CMS options may be preferable where they reduce maintenance.

The network should share a canonical NYF content record so material is not duplicated blindly across properties. Each published item should retain provenance, project ownership, publication destination and reuse rules.

## Required corpus metadata

For every recovered piece, capture:

- title or working title
- source location
- date/version when known
- project or research lineage
- topic/search territory
- status: fragment, research, draft, finished, published
- privacy: private, source-only, public candidate, public
- relationships: what it draws from and what it later produced
- possible destination property

## First experiment

Do not launch dozens of sites.

1. Complete the corpus map.
2. Select one vertical with the deepest existing inventory.
3. Build one focused publication property.
4. Publish a minimum viable body of substantive material.
5. Measure Google Search Console impressions/clicks later, after the content base exists.
6. Use the results to decide whether to replicate the model.

## Current priority

The immediate task is retrieval and corpus mapping. Search/discovery infrastructure and monetization come after the body of work is identified and organized.
