Source order.

Primary sources first

Research papers, official datasets and authoritative documentation establish the claim boundary.

Separate evidence levels

A mechanism, correlation, clinical outcome and personal account are not treated as interchangeable.

Name uncertainty

Open questions and disagreement remain visible instead of being edited into confidence.

Publish after review

The public page comes after the source check, not before it.
