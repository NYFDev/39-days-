method.

Retrieve what became true. Preserve the source. Interpret without replacing the record. Publish only the reviewed public layer.

Retrieval before reconstruction.

Memory compresses sequence and assigns meaning early. The method begins with recoverable records: time, files, communication, receipts and version history.

Narrative follows the evidence. It does not substitute for it.
